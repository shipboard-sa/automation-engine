Signals How-To Library

Lee Hutchins and Claude · SV Unladen Swallow 2 · November 2026

Hardware you already own, signals you didn’t know you had. Each piece follows the same structure: what the signal provides, what it takes physically, how to read it in software, and lessons learned. Short, standalone, reproducible.

Your Router Already Knows Who’s Aboard

Crew presence detection via the router’s client-list API. One authenticated call per cycle, match by DHCP-reserved IP, 10-minute window for dozing phones.

A $20 Dinghy Alarm That Lets You Sleep

Wire loop through the painter, a digital input, a physical arm switch. Immediate theft detection for the cost of a wire and a magnetic reed switch.

Your Camera Already Knows Who’s on the Stern

Object detection via Frigate or camera-hub AI. Zone-based person events over MQTT, dwell-time filtering, day/night considerations.

Your Stereo Already Has Speakers Everywhere

Four ways to push alarm audio through a Fusion stereo: NMEA 2000 + line-in, DLNA, Bluetooth, or line-in only. Volume management, power sequencing, music priority.

A $3 Clamp That Tells You Everything

Non-invasive DC current sensing on any circuit. Bilge pump cycles, freshwater pump leak detection, runtime trending. One conductor only.

Your Dish Already Knows Why You’re Offline

gRPC at 192.168.100.1:9200, unauthenticated. Dish state, obstruction fraction, throughput, and the two-device distinction (dish vs router).
